# Code of conduct

HelioField is a small open-source project for the heliophysics community. Everyone who takes part, in issues, discussions, pull requests or anywhere else the project meets, is expected to help keep it welcoming and useful.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the field, to open source or to the language of the discussion.
- Giving and receiving constructive criticism about the work, not the person.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.
- Crediting the work, data and ideas of others.
- Respecting that people contribute in their own time and in different ways.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity, background, career stage or institution.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the GitHub repository and its issues, pull requests and discussions, and any other channel where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please write to the maintainer, Soumyaranjan Dash, at <sdash@nso.edu>. Reports are read in confidence, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and respond in the way that seems fair and proportionate. Depending on the case, that may be a private note, a public request to stop, the removal of comments or contributions, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code may be edited or removed.
