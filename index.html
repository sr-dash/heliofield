What to download

Every zip has the Python scripts (plot the maps, plot the 3D view, recompute the corona) and a README. File names carry the model date and today's date.

What is in the files

Compute the field for ?

There is no ready-made product for this date. Your browser will download the SDO/HMI map for (about 90 kB) and compute the field, field lines, current sheet and spacecraft connections itself.

  • The field shown now will be cleared from memory. Going back to it means loading it again.
  • Please wait for the new map. It usually appears within seconds, but can take 2–3 minutes on a slow connection or an older phone or tablet.
  • A rotation map is built from about 27 days of observations around this date, so it shows the field around the date, not on that exact day. Earth and the spacecraft are placed for 12:00 UTC on the date.
  • No rotation map covers this exact date, so the nearest one is used.
