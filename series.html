Each point is one Carrington rotation (about 27 days): an SDO/HMI synoptic map, extrapolated with the same PFSS model the viewer uses (source surface at 2.5 R☉). Hover or tap a chart to read every series at that rotation, or use the arrow keys once a chart has focus. How these are computed

Open flux at the source surface

Unsigned magnetic flux through the source surface, 1022 Mx. In-situ estimates at 1 au are typically about twice the PFSS value: the open flux problem (Linker et al. 2017).

Open-field area

Share of the photosphere whose field reaches the source surface, %: the model's coronal holes. Shown on the Sun with Open-field footprints in the viewer.

Current-sheet tilt

Highest latitude reached by the source-surface neutral line in each hemisphere, degrees, as in the Wilcox Solar Observatory product. The grid tops out at 84°.

Polar field

Mean photospheric Br poleward of 60°, gauss. The sign changes mark the polar reversals at solar maximum.
